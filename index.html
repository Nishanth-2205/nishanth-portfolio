Publications

Research that turned into usable systems.

Two sign language recognition publications: one implementation-focused journal article and one review chapter that framed the solution space.

Journal Article

Intelligent Sign Language Recognition Using Deep Sequential Architectures in Keras

International Journal of Computing and Digital Systems, University of Bahrain, 2026

The journal article presents a real-time American Sign Language recognition system that translates word-level gestures into text and speech through MediaPipe hand landmarks, a Keras Sequential model, TensorFlow, and a Flask web interface. It focuses on practical assistive communication, using a roughly 31,000-row dataset across 30 classes and 42 hand landmarks to reduce irrelevant visual bias while reporting 99% accuracy.

Read journal article

Book Chapter

A Review on Sign Language Recognition Techniques

Springer Nature Singapore, Proceedings of the 2nd International Conference on Cognitive and Intelligent Computing, 2023

The Springer book chapter reviews sign language recognition techniques developed to help people who rely on signing communicate with people who may not understand sign language. It compares glove-based and computer-vision-based approaches, discussing their methodologies, advantages, and tradeoffs as the field moves toward text and speech output systems.
