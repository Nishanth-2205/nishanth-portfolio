EY Case Competition 2025

Strategic AI Transformation

Helios Capital Group

Project Overview

This was a team-based consulting project developed for the EY Case Competition 2025, focused on Helios Capital Group, a global, systemically important financial institution managing trillions in assets and facing rapid AI-driven disruption across wealth management, lending, insurance, and payments. The objective was to design a disciplined AI transformation strategy that would enable Helios to achieve a sustainable 15% Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE) within five years while preserving regulatory trust, operational stability, and long-term brand equity.

My Role - Nishanth Ganji | Technology Consultant

I served as a Technology Consultant, leading the design of the AI-enabled technology and data architecture. I worked closely with strategy teammates to translate business objectives, financial constraints, and regulatory requirements into an execution-ready digital roadmap.

My Approach

My work focused on transforming Helios's strategic mandate into a scalable, enterprise-grade AI operating model. I designed a phased transformation that unified enterprise data on a modern cloud platform, embedded AI directly into advisor workflows, and established governance and compliance controls to ensure regulatory alignment. The solution emphasized advisor augmentation, ensuring AI delivered real-time, explainable, and highly personalized insights while maintaining Helios's conservative risk culture.

Key Contributions

  • Architected an AI-driven wealth management platform integrating enterprise data, real-time analytics, and governed AI services to deliver personalized portfolio intelligence and automated risk management.
  • Designed a mandatory AI adoption and change framework combining phased pilots, performance incentives, executive oversight, and compliance logging to accelerate enterprise-wide transformation.
  • Built a 24-month phased implementation roadmap covering governance, data integration, AI pilots, enterprise scaling, and continuous optimization.
  • Led financial modeling projecting $4.2B in cost savings, 16.97% target ROTCE, and 70-80% advisor capacity uplift within 18-24 months.

Outcome

The final deliverable provided Helios leadership with a credible, regulator-ready transformation roadmap that converted fragmented legacy operations into an AI-empowered wealth platform, stabilized market confidence, and positioned the organization to exceed its 15% ROTCE target through sustained efficiency gains, higher fee income, and long-term digital competitiveness.

Sign Language Recognition for Inclusivity

Research + Product Build

Description

Led a team of three in developing a Python-based sign language recognition website, surpassing existing solutions by handling 130% more classes and achieving a 4.5% increase in accuracy through multiclass classification, an artificial neural network (Tensorflow & Keras), and Flask.

Bias Reduction

Eliminated bias by training the model solely on hand landmark positions, allowing it to generalize across hands of varying skin colors and sizes.

Communication + Publication

Delivered a technical presentation to a technical audience and published the research in Springer. DOI:10.1007/978-981-99-2742-5_32.

About Me

"The Force will be with you. Always."

That line from Star Wars is not about hope. It is about commitment. It means when things get difficult, when systems fail, when pressure rises, you do not disappear. You stay. You adapt. You finish what you started.

That is how I build.

I do not chase trends in technology. I build systems that endure. My journey began in engineering, sharpened in enterprise development, and now evolves at Indiana University's Kelley School of Business, where I pursue my Master's in Information Systems with a perfect 4.0 GPA while serving as a Graduate Assistant for academic excellence. But the real story is not the credentials. It is the mindset behind them. I am obsessed with understanding how complex systems work and unwilling to tolerate inefficiency in any form.

At Accelerize360, I rose from developer to Senior Developer by doing the hard things well and doing them consistently. I lead enterprise CRM architecture, design multi-cloud solutions for Fortune 500 clients, and deliver platforms executives trust when the stakes are high. I have built real-time performance dashboards that shape strategy, engineered backend optimizations that transformed system speed and reliability, and mentored over forty engineers to ship better software with confidence and discipline.

But engineering was never the endgame.

I published AI research with Springer and led the creation of a real-time Sign Language Recognition system with ninety-nine percent accuracy, not for recognition, but to prove that technology should solve problems that matter. From neural networks to deployed platforms, I have taken ideas from nothing to production systems that change real lives.

Leadership did not arrive with a title. It was forged in execution. I have led cross-functional teams, built governance frameworks, and owned products from first requirement to post-launch adoption. When complexity rises, I do not panic. I simplify. I align people. I execute. I speak the language of engineers, executives, and users, and I make sure they are moving toward the same future.

Today my focus spans AI systems, enterprise platforms, cloud architecture, and product strategy. I do not just write code. I design leverage. I do not manage projects. I build momentum. I do not chase success. I construct it.

This portfolio is not a highlight reel. It is a record of pressure faced, problems solved, systems built, teams elevated, and standards raised.

And the mission is clear. We are only getting started.
